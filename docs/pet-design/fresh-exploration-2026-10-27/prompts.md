## living-companions.png

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original desktop companion character exploration board for Aieyes, a desktop command center that lets users watch AI coding agents working, waiting for input, and completing tasks.
Primary request: design THREE genuinely distinct, memorable original desktop pet candidates, as a premium character design presentation. Completely fresh creative exploration, no reference images. These should feel like charming adult creative-tool companions, with personality conveyed through silhouette, posture, and expressive faces; not generic identical blobs with huge eyes.
Composition: landscape 3:2 image, three equal spacious vertical columns on warm ivory background. Thin subtle dividing rules. Small top title exactly "Aieyes / Living companions". Columns labeled exactly "01", "02", "03" in restrained typography. Each column has one large full-body three-quarter hero character in the upper/middle area and two much smaller full-body expressive poses below, leaving air between silhouettes. Each candidate must stay exactly consistent between its three poses. Full bodies unobstructed. No props floating around the board, no UI, no extra text, no watermark.
01: an original squat folded-ear scout creature, ink-blue soft fur with cream face and one single coral ear tip, broad angular folded ears make a distinctive horizontally stretched silhouette, short feet, tiny confident eyes with eyebrows, oversized soft tail curved beside body like a comma. Alert but quietly mischievous. Hero stands listening; small poses lean forward focused and raise one paw requesting attention. Not a recognizable existing character, not a conventional cat or rabbit.
02: a gentle cloak moth, dusty plum and butter-yellow, compact upright body, two broad folded wings form a little cape, short curved antennae, small bright attentive eyes. Its silhouette is triangular and upright, clearly unlike 01. Hero wings folded; small poses unfold wings in a happy completion gesture and wrap wings around itself sleeping. Elegant stylized creature with opaque graphic wings, no hairy insect realism, no scary insect legs.
03: a grounded little garden tortoise, sage-green limbs and an ochre smoothly faceted pebble shell, a single tiny sprout growing from shell, expressive relaxed half-moon eyes, rounded square head. Horizontally low silhouette, chunky feet. Hero takes a step; small poses tuck in with sleepy head and stretch head up excitedly, sprout tilts with expression.
Style: polished hand-painted 2.5D editorial character illustration, soft gouache grain, confident clean edges, subtle tactile shading, restrained sophisticated colors. All three same rendering language and similar visual scale, but radically different construction and silhouette. They must read clearly as 64 to 96 pixel desktop companions. Avoid microdetail, excessive glossy toy rendering, accessories carrying identity, generic robots with screen faces, oversized glass eyes. Soft minimal contact shadows only. High art-direction quality.

## digital-familiars.png

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: original desktop companion character exploration board for Aieyes, a desktop command center that helps users observe AI coding agents working, waiting for input, and completing tasks.
Primary request: design THREE distinct original digital-life desktop pet candidates as premium character concept art. Fresh character exploration without reference images. Combine memorable silhouettes, small-scale readability, curiosity and quiet competence. Not three variations on one blob, and not conventional robot heads.
Composition: landscape 3:2 board, three spacious equal vertical columns, pale cool-gray paper background, very subtle divider rules. Small top title exactly "Aieyes / Digital familiars". Columns labeled exactly "04", "05", "06" in restrained typography. One large full-body three-quarter hero in each column and two smaller full-body emotion/action poses below. Every small pose must maintain the exact character construction and colors of its hero. All edges visible with plenty of empty space. No other text, no UI, no environmental scene, no watermark.
04: a magnetic-ring messenger. An open horseshoe-shaped deep cobalt body with contrasting orange magnet end caps, a little face with two ivory dot eyes on the upper curved arch, two tiny mitten hands and short springy dark legs. The open negative space inside the horseshoe is essential to the silhouette. Not a sphere inside a ring, not a robot screen. Hero energetic confident stance; small poses arch body forward thinking and throw both hands up in delight, keeping open horseshoe shape. Friendly industrial charm, one bold graphic continuous body.
05: an animated folded-paper swimming creature, ivory and soft vermilion. It is an abstract miniature flying fish made from three or four large folded planes, asymmetric high triangular dorsal fin and broad folded tail; two little ink eyes integrated into front face. No visible paper writing. Its crisp swept-back silhouette should look elegant and mischievous, a small aerial companion rather than a decoration. Hero hovers three-quarter; small poses curl toward a point of interest and settle with fin folded for sleep. Keep character anatomy consistent across poses, big legible planes, not intricate origami.
06: an ink-drop artisan. A small near-black fluid creature with a single swept-up brush-tip crest, pear-shaped body, expressive cream crescent eyes, short substantial legs and two flexible little arms; subtle cobalt edge highlights, one small ivory patch on belly. The crest and expressive squashing are its main identity. No hat, no tools or accessories. Hero stands with hands on hips and curious tilted head; small poses stretch into a forward busy stride and flatten into a sleepy puddle while retaining eyes and crest. Friendly, witty, not spooky.
Style: beautiful stylized 2.5D designer character illustration with matte ceramic-like shading and slightly textured editorial finish. Crisp deliberate contour, restrained color, very soft shadow immediately beneath characters. Comparable quality and scale across all three, distinct materials and construction. Thoughtful collectible character appeal for adults, not photorealistic toy product photographs. Strong readable silhouette at 64 to 96 pixels. Avoid tiny decoration, oversized huge glass eyes, busy scenery, heavy gradients, generic screen-face robots, familiar existing IP.
