## DeepSeek balance split and quota detail columns

Changes since v0.2.2:

- Split DeepSeek balance rings into granted and paid arcs, showing both amounts and their share in the ring center, tiles and accessibility descriptions.
- Show Qoder credit ring halves with their remaining credits and percentage instead of a combined total.
- Restructure compact quota details into primary metrics and facts columns: quota windows and Qoder credit bars beside valuations, balances and analysis, collapsing to one column when space is tight.
- Use compact valuation summaries and tighter detail rows, and move the analysis entry into the facts column.
- Let quota cards stretch with a taller facts column and stay at their natural height otherwise.

## 中文

相较 v0.2.2：

- DeepSeek 余额圆环拆分为赠送与充值两段弧线，圆环中心、磁贴与无障碍描述分别显示两部分金额及占比。
- Qoder Credits 圆环上下两段分别显示剩余额度与百分比，不再合并为单一合计。
- 紧凑额度详情重构为「主指标 + 事实」双列：额度周期与 Qoder Credits 额度条在一侧，估值、余额与分析在另一侧，空间不足时自动切换单列。
- 估值摘要使用紧凑金额，详情行更紧凑，“查看分析”入口移入事实列。
- 额度卡片可随更高的事实列拉伸，否则保持自然高度。
