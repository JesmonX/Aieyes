## Qoder credit rings and compact quota details

Changes since v0.2.1:

- Show Qoder and Qoder CN credits as two fixed halves: addon credits on top and plan credits below, in the main window, floating panel, menu bar and tray, including a compact plan+addon total.
- Add compact quota details to the ring styles, with per-window remaining amounts, valuations and reset times without repeating the overview bars.
- Use short labels for long model group names and omit the subscription badge on grouped tiles.
- Preserve explicit Qoder reset times instead of deriving month boundaries, and accept variable-precision Antigravity token expiry timestamps.

## 中文

相较 v0.2.1：

- Qoder 和 Qoder CN 的 Credits 圆环固定分为上下两段：上半圈 addon、下半圈 plan，覆盖主窗口、悬浮面板、macOS 菜单栏和 Windows/Linux 托盘，并显示 plan+addon 合计。
- 圆环样式新增紧凑额度详情，按周期显示剩余额度、估值和重置时间，不再重复概览额度条。
- 较长的模型分组名使用短标签，分组磁贴不再重复显示订阅徽章。
- 保留 Qoder 明确返回的重置时间，不再根据到期时间推算月初；Antigravity 登录有效期支持不同精度的 RFC3339 时间戳。
