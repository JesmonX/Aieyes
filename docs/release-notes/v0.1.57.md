## Release scope

This release covers **v0.1.56 → v0.1.57**, adding session sounds and improving panel layout, Codex account switching, and account history recovery.

## Session sounds

- Add separate sounds for sessions that need input and tasks that complete, with independent switches and preview controls on macOS, Windows, and Linux.
- Play sounds for fresh state changes while the panel is closed, without replaying old alerts after startup, repeated polling, or a temporary monitoring interruption. Volume follows the system output.

## Panel and account layout

- Correct the first macOS panel layout and resize it to fit the available display area.
- Repair Windows and Linux panel dimensions after opening or a display scale change, and wait for fonts and layout before reporting that the panel is ready.
- Keep collapsed subscription account cards at a shared height as quota content changes, including cards awaiting their first quota query.

## Codex accounts and history

- Allow account switching without waiting for ordinary quota and model queries to finish. Cancel outdated requests and prevent their results from overwriting the selected account, including after switching away and back.
- Keep credential writes coordinated, with a bounded wait and a specific diagnostic when another credential operation prevents switching.
- Recheck affected processes during confirmed account switches, handle related login processes that appear after confirmation, and preserve instances with independent API credentials.
- Record the observed login identity around quota queries without treating a queried inactive profile as the active login.
- Add account-specific, bounded Codex history recovery with a dry-run option and separate replay baselines. Skip intervals with unproven device coverage or overlap with reliable existing segments, and extend anonymous diagnostics for attribution gaps.

## Regression coverage

- Extend native and browser checks for session sounds and account card layout, plus core and remote account-switching regressions.
- Update desktop interaction tests to verify the panel-open request followed by its readiness notification, while retaining drag suppression and keyboard activation checks.
