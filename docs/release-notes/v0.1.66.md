## Desktop layout and overlays

- Daily and annual usage tables have clearer alignment; heatmap month labels and day cells align across short and partial ranges.
- Account selection remains usable with long names and narrow windows. Machine menus offer concise login and account-switch actions with more useful initial directories.
- GPU utilization at 100% keeps its normal category color, while capacity warnings remain in place.
- Windows pets and panels better retain their topmost position without taking keyboard focus. macOS pets and pinned popovers restore their intended ordering when the app activates.
